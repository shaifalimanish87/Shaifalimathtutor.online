About Shaifali Jain

A patient, structured and caring online math tutor for students worldwide.

My name is Shaifali Jain, and I am a dedicated and passionate math educator with experience teaching students across different grades and curricula. Helping students who find math intimidating is what I love doing most.

My goal is simple: to help every student build a strong foundation, gain real confidence, and enjoy learning mathematics rather than fearing it. I believe every student can master math with the right guidance, patience and encouragement.

Curricula I Teach

  • Indian Curriculum (CBSE / ICSE, Grades 6–10)
  • American Curriculum (Pre-Algebra, Algebra 1 & 2, Geometry)
  • UK Curriculum (KS2, KS3, GCSE)
  • Australian Curriculum & general homework help

How My Classes Work

Lessons are live, one-on-one video sessions on a digital whiteboard. You only need a laptop or tablet, a stable internet connection and a notebook. Timings are flexible across time zones.

My Teaching Approach

A four-step method designed to build real understanding, not just memorization.

  1. 1

    Understand the Need

    Before the first class, I talk with parents and students to understand exactly where extra attention is needed and set clear learning goals.

  2. 2

    I Do

    I introduce new concepts using interactive strategies that activate prior knowledge and spark curiosity.

  3. 3

    We Do

    We work through examples together, building confidence step by step until the concept feels natural.

  4. 4

    You Do

    Students show their understanding independently through guided practice, quizzes and homework.
