Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about classes, scheduling, homework and fees.

How does online math tutoring work?

Classes are conducted over video call using a digital whiteboard. All you need is a laptop or tablet, a stable internet connection, and a quiet space to learn.

What devices or supplies are needed?

A laptop, tablet or computer with a camera and microphone, along with a notebook and pen for practice. A stylus is helpful but not required.

Which grade levels do you teach?

I teach students from primary grades through high school, across CBSE/ICSE, American, UK and Australian curricula.

How often are sessions held?

Session frequency is flexible, from once a week to daily sessions, based on the student's needs and goals, decided together during the consultation.

What is the homework policy?

Homework is assigned after most sessions to reinforce concepts. It is reviewed at the start of the next class to track progress and clear doubts.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please provide at least 24 hours' notice to reschedule or cancel a session so the time slot can be offered to another student.

How much do the classes cost?

Fees depend on grade, curriculum and number of sessions. Tap Get Instant Price on the Pricing page and the price is shared on WhatsApp.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. The first consultation is free so we can understand your child's needs and see if we are a good match.

Can you teach students in other time zones?

Yes. Timings are flexible, and I teach students in India and abroad.

Still have a question? Contact me or message on WhatsApp.
