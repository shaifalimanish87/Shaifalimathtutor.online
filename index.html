My Teaching Approach

A four-step method designed to build real understanding, not just memorization.

  1. 1

    Understand the Need

    Before the first class, I talk with parents and students to understand exactly where extra attention is needed and set clear learning goals.

  2. 2

    I Do

    I introduce new concepts using interactive strategies that activate prior knowledge and spark curiosity.

  3. 3

    We Do

    We work through examples together, building confidence step by step until the concept feels natural.

  4. 4

    You Do

    Students show their understanding independently through guided practice, quizzes and homework.
