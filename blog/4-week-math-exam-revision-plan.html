A Simple 4-Week Math Exam Revision Plan

By Shaifali Jain · 26 October 2026

Good exam results come from steady, planned revision, not last-night cramming. Here is a simple plan you can adapt to any board or grade.

Week 1: List and diagnose

Write down every chapter in the syllabus. Mark each one green (confident), yellow (okay) or red (weak). Spend the most time on red topics.

Week 2: Fix the weak topics

Revisit the concept first, then solve textbook examples, then try exercise questions. Keep a notebook of formulas and common mistakes.

Week 3: Practise past papers

Solve at least two full papers under timed conditions. Check your answers and note why you lost marks: concept, calculation or time.

Week 4: Revise and relax

Go through your formula notebook, re-do mistakes from earlier papers and sleep well. Avoid learning new topics in the last two days.

Exam day tips

  • Read the whole paper first and start with questions you know.
  • Show all steps for method marks.
  • Keep 10 minutes at the end to check.
