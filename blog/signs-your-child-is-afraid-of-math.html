6 Signs Your Child Is Afraid of Math (and How to Help)

By Shaifali Jain · 12 October 2026

Many children say "I'm bad at math" long before they have actually struggled with it. Fear of math usually comes from a few missed basics or a bad experience in class. The good news is that it can be fixed.

Common signs

  • They avoid or delay math homework.
  • They say things like "I can't do it" before even trying.
  • They hurry to finish without checking the answer.
  • They feel stomach aches or stress before math tests.
  • They memorise steps but cannot explain why they work.
  • Marks in math drop while other subjects stay steady.

How parents can help

Praise effort, not just marks. Say "I liked how you kept trying" instead of "You got it right."

Go back to the basics. Gaps in fractions, tables or negative numbers cause trouble in algebra later. Fixing the base fixes the fear.

Make mistakes normal. Treat a wrong answer as information about what to learn next.

Use real-life math. Cooking, shopping and travel plans show children that math is useful.

Get patient support. A tutor who explains the same idea in different ways, without hurry, can change how a child feels about the subject. Read about my teaching approach or talk to me.
